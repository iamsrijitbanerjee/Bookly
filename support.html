Frequently Asked Questions

Are the books really 100% free?

Yes. Our core mission is to democratize education. All standard NCERT textbooks and public domain literature hosted on Bookly will always be free to read and download without any hidden fees or paywalls.

Why isn't the "Read Online" feature working for me?

The "Read Online" feature relies on your browser's native PDF viewer. If it forces a download instead of opening a new tab, check your browser settings to ensure PDF viewing is enabled, or try using a modern browser like Chrome or Edge.

Can I request a specific book?

Absolutely! Head over to our Explore page and use the "Suggest a Book" widget. We review suggestions weekly and prioritize based on curriculum demand.

Do I need to create an account to save custom playlists?

No! Your custom playlists and favorites are saved directly to your device's local storage. This means your data remains private and loads instantly, though clearing your browser cache will reset your folders.
