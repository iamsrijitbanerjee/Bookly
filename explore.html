The Mission

Democratizing Education for All

At Bookly, we believe that a lack of financial resources should never stand in the way of a student's potential. Textbooks are expensive, and accessing high-quality curriculums can be a major hurdle for many.

Our mission is simple: to provide a sleek, fast, and completely free hub for essential educational materials. By bringing together NCERT texts and classic literature into one organized platform, we aim to empower students to learn on their own terms, anywhere in the world.

The Journey

Phase 1 • 2022

The Side Quest Begins

Bookly started as a humble side project. Built over a few weekends, it was a simple HTML page designed to help a small group of peers find textbooks easily without hitting paywalls or dealing with sketchy ad-filled download sites.

Phase 2 • 2024

Expanding the Library

As word spread, the demand grew. We expanded the catalog to include over 15+ official NCERT curriculums, spanning Mathematics, Science, and English for middle and high school students across multiple regions.

Phase 3 • 2026

The Modern Hub Rebirth

Realizing the potential to help thousands more, Bookly was completely architected. We migrated to a dynamic JSON database, integrated a native in-browser PDF reader, and built personalized study playlists—transforming a simple webpage into a fully-fledged web application.
